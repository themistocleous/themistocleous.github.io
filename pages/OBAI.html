Open Brain AI & Digital Clinical Tools

Translating computational neurolinguistics, deep learning, and acoustic signal processing into scalable, open, and clinically validated software platforms for researchers, clinicians, and educators.

Flagship Computational Platform

Open Brain AI (OBAI)

Founded and architected by Prof. Charalambos Themistocleous, Open Brain AI (openbrainai.com) is a cloud-based artificial intelligence platform that automates multi-level speech, language, and cognitive assessment across 15+ languages. By coupling deep neural networks, large language models, and acoustic-phonetic pipelines, OBAI transforms raw spoken and written productions into objective, reproducible digital biomarkers.

Clinical & Scientific Rationale

Human speech and language production is one of the most computationally demanding neurocognitive operations performed by the brain, recruiting distributed networks across perisylvian, frontostriatal, and temporoparietal cortices. When neurological conditions—such as Alzheimer's disease (AD), Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI), Primary Progressive Aphasia (PPA), post-stroke aphasia, traumatic brain injury, or neurodevelopmental conditions (Autism, DLD)—affect brain networks, subtle acoustic and linguistic alterations emerge long before standard clinical tests detect decline.

Traditional manual transcription and clinical scoring are labor-intensive, time-consuming, and susceptible to inter-rater variability. Open Brain AI bridges computational neurolinguistics and clinical practice by providing an end-to-end automated pipeline that extracts hundreds of standardized phonetic, prosodic, morphosyntactic, lexical-semantic, and discourse-level measures in seconds.

Core Architecture & Modules of Open Brain AI

Module 01 • Automated Transcription & Discourse Editor

OBAI Text & Speech Editor

An integrated multimodal workspace supporting speech-to-text transcription, automated segmentation of audio into words, syllables, vowels, and consonants, and interactive text editing for clinical narratives, picture descriptions, and semi-spontaneous interviews.

Speech-to-Text ASR Forced Alignment IPA Transcription
Module 02 • Multi-Level Linguistic & Acoustic Engine

Comprehensive Language Analysis Pipeline

Extracts objective biomarkers across all levels of grammar and speech: phonetics & voice quality ($f_0$, jitter, shimmer, HNR, vowel formants, pause dynamics), phonology, morphology & part-of-speech ratios, syntactic complexity, and lexical-semantic density.

Prosody & Voice Quality Morphosyntax Information-Theoretic Metrics
Module 03 • Automated Clinical Scoring Suite

Advanced Clinical Assessment Tools

Houses specialized, peer-reviewed clinical scoring applications—including automated Spelling Error Scoring (words and pseudowords), Phonological Error Distance, and Semantic Similarity & Fluency Scoring—validated on neurological cohorts.

Spelling Scoring Phonological Distance Semantic Vectors
Module 04 • Decision Support & Normative Benchmarking

OBAI Clinical & Educational Companion

Generates interpretable diagnostic reports comparing individual patient or student profiles against normative clinical language distributions across tasks, supporting early screening, differential subtyping, longitudinal monitoring, and therapy target detection.

Digital Tools & Open-Source Software Portfolio

In addition to the integrated Open Brain AI cloud environment, Prof. Themistocleous has developed and released open-source clinical applications, computational linguistics tools, acoustic analysis scripts, and lexical databases for the research community:

01 • Cloud AI Platform • Web Application

Open Brain AI Platform

End-to-end computational platform providing researchers, clinicians, and educators with automated speech transcription, vowel/consonant acoustic segmentation, and multi-level linguistic analysis (phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics) via an accessible web interface.

openbrainai.com Frontiers in Human Neuroscience (2024)
02 • Clinical Assessment Tool • Python / OBAI Module

Automated Spelling Scoring Application

Computes automated, fine-grained orthographic and phonological error metrics on real words and pseudowords in patients with neurological disorders (e.g., PPA and post-stroke dysgraphia). Integrated into Open Brain AI, with a standalone GUI available for local clinical use.

GitHub Repository JSLHR (2020)
03 • Clinical Assessment Tool • Python / OBAI Module

Automated Phonology Scoring Application

Quantifies phonological production accuracy and feature-weighted phonemic edit distances in developmental and acquired speech disorders (validated in Swedish-speaking children with DLD and adults with aphasia). Integrated into Open Brain AI with a local GUI option.

GitHub Repository
04 • Clinical Assessment Tool • OBAI Module

Semantics Scoring Application

Automated computational tool for scoring lexical-semantic similarity, semantic clustering, and conceptual coherence in clinical naming, recall, and connected discourse tasks. Currently deployed within Open Brain AI (standalone GUI available upon request).

Integrated in Open Brain AI
05 • Clinical & Psycholinguistic Testing • Web/Desktop App

Clinical Tool: Word Repetition App

Digital clinical assessment tool designed for administering, recording, and scoring word repetition tasks in patients with motor speech disorders, apraxia of speech, and aphasia.

GitHub Repository
06 • Working Memory & Clinical Testing • Web/Desktop App

Clinical Tool: Word Repetition Span App

Automated experimental and clinical application for evaluating verbal short-term memory and phonological working memory span through graded word repetition sequences.

GitHub Repository
07 • Cross-Platform Desktop Software • Mac / Linux / Windows

NLP Editor & Phonetics IPA Converter

Cross-platform text editor facilitating linguistic preprocessing, tokenization, and automated grapheme-to-phoneme conversion into the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) across macOS, Linux, and Windows (evolving from the earlier Windows Phonetics IPA utility).

GitHub Repository
08 • Acoustic Phonetics & Signal Processing • Praat

Acoustic Analysis Praat Scripts Library

Curated open-source collection of Praat scripts for automated extraction of vowel formants ($F_1$–$F_4$), fundamental frequency ($f_0$) contours, spectral moments of fricatives and sonorants, stop burst acoustics, and prosodic duration metrics.

GitHub Repository
09 • Computational Lexicography & Speech Synthesis

Web Dictionary of Cypriot Greek (Syndychies / LexCy)

Online lexical database and reverse dictionary of Cypriot Greek featuring an automated IPA transcription converter algorithm, morphological annotation, and text-to-speech synthesis (presented at EURALEX 2012, Oslo).

GitHub Repository
10 • Linguistic Typography & Fieldwork Utilities

IPA, Polytonic & Dialectal Keyboard Layouts

Custom system keyboard layouts for rapid, standards-compliant input of the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA), Dialectal Greek, and Greek Polytonic orthography for linguists and philologists.

GitHub Repository
