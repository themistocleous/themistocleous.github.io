Open Brain AI

Pioneering Computational Language Assessment to decode the connection between the brain, language, and speech pathology.

The Vision

Human speech is one of the most complex cognitive tasks we perform, making it a profound digital window into the health of the brain. When the brain undergoes structural or functional changes—such as in neurodegenerative diseases like dementia—speech and language patterns degrade in subtle, measurable ways long before standard clinical symptoms appear. Similarly, language measures can reflect the complex changes during the long period of language acquisition or language learning.

Open Brain AI was founded to capture and quantify these acoustic and linguistic footprints. By bridging deep academic research with scalable software engineering, we translate raw speech data into actionable, objective clinical and educational metrics.
