Themistocleous C. (2015). Experimental Design and Statistics in Linguistics. Using R (In Greek). Athens: Ion. ISBN:978-960-508-187-4.

This book is an introduction to experimental methodology and statistics using R and aims:
  • to present the basic principles of experimental methodology and experimental design.
  • to introduce statistics and their application to language problems.
  • to introduce R, a programming language with specialized functions for statistical analysis.
