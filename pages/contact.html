Opportunities for Collaboration & Supervision

European & International Consortia

ERC, Horizon Europe, MSCA & Clinical Trials

We actively partner with neurology departments, memory clinics, speech-language pathology centers, and computational linguistics groups on ERC, Horizon Europe, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships), NIH, and Nordic/RCN grant initiatives. Our laboratory and the Open Brain AI platform provide scalable infrastructure for multicenter digital biomarker extraction across 15+ languages.

Doctoral, Postdoctoral & Master's Candidates

Prospective PhD Fellows, Postdocs & MA Students

Prospective PhD candidates, MSCA/RCN postdoctoral fellows, and Master's students at the University of Oslo interested in AI-driven speech and language assessment, aphasia, dementia, developmental language disorders, or experimental phonetics are encouraged to reach out with a CV and brief research statement.
